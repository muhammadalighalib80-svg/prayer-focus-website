Core Principles & Architecture

🛡️

100% Offline Prayer Engine

Calculates Fajr, Zuhr, Asr, Maghrib, and Isha times offline directly on your processor using astronomical equations. No internet permission required to calculate prayer times.

🔒

Strict Accessibility Firewall

Enforces canRetrieveWindowContent = false. Android's Accessibility Service is used solely to identify the active package name in transient RAM. Keystrokes, screen text, chats, and personal data are never inspected or saved.

⏱️

Scheduled 10-Minute Windows

When prayer time arrives, Prayer Focus activates a gentle 10-minute focus window. Selected distracting apps are temporarily restricted so you can pray uninterrupted.

🚨

Emergency & Family Safety

Emergency dialers, phone call services, and WhatsApp are permanently exempted from restriction to ensure family reachability during urgent situations.

⚡

Background Reliability

Engineered for modern Android versions (Android 8 to 15) with exact alarms, reboot self-healing, and vendor battery optimization guidance.

📱

Private Sandboxed Storage

Your selected apps, city configurations, and prayer preferences stay sealed on your phone in private Jetpack DataStore preferences.
